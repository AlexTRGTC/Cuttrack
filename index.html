Personalisation

Eight-week programme setup

Start a new eight-week cycle whenever you are ready. Previous weigh-ins, food, activity and completed workouts are retained as history.

Flexible weekly training schedule

Move workouts A, B, C and D to whichever days suit you. Runs are scheduled separately. Rest days are not treated as missed sessions when you meet your weekly training plan.

Starting a new cycle changes the active programme dates and schedule only. It does not erase your previous records. Earlier programme cycles remain available in the history export.
